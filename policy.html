Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to BuyCAN ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the BuyCAN iOS app collects, why, and who it is shared with.

In short: BuyCAN has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number. The app does not read your location, contacts or photos. We do not sell your data, and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information We Collect

An anonymous device identifier. The app sends Apple's per-app device identifier (not your name or your advertising identifier) with the requests below. It lets us apply the daily limit on new products, show your impact counts and send you notifications. It changes if you delete BuyCAN.

Products you look up. The barcodes you scan and the product names you search for, together with the device identifier.

App usage events. For example, whether a list of Canadian alternatives was shown or tapped, and which app version you use.

Notifications, if you turn them on. Your device's push token and time zone, so notifications such as the weekly recap arrive at a sensible local time.

Feedback you send. The text and topic you write in the feedback form. It is not linked to your device identifier. Please don't include personal details you don't want us to have.

Advertising

BuyCAN shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as device identifiers, approximate location based on your IP address, the ads you see and tap, and diagnostic data, as described in Google's policy. BuyCAN does not ask for permission to track you, so iOS does not share your advertising identifier (IDFA) with Google.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to run the app (looking up products, finding Canadian alternatives, keeping daily limits fair), to show your impact counts, to send notifications you asked for, to understand which features are useful, and to fix problems and prevent abuse. Products that are researched for the first time are added to our product database so everyone can look them up; that product information is not personal.

Third Party Services

We work with these services to run BuyCAN:

  • OpenAI and Perplexity research products we don't know yet. They receive product names and barcodes only, never your device identifier.
  • Google AdMob shows ads (see Advertising above).
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications.
  • Cloud hosting providers (Render and Microsoft Azure) host our servers, database and product images.

These services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Your Choices

You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings. Deleting BuyCAN resets the device identifier, so new activity can't be connected to old activity. To ask a question or make a request about your data, contact us using the details below.

Data Retention and Security

We keep information for as long as we need it to run and improve BuyCAN. It is not linked to your name or contact details. We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, but no system is completely secure.

Children

BuyCAN is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date below.

How it works

  1. Enter your product's barcode (UPC or EAN) to see what BuyCAN shows today.
  2. Correct what's wrong, or fill in a product we don't have yet.
  3. Add links that back up any Canadian claim, such as a photo of the label or your product page.
  4. Our team reviews every submission by hand and may email you to confirm you work for the brand. Nothing changes in the app until we approve it.

It's free. We don't accept file uploads: please link to images and pages hosted on your own website. Information we approve is shown in the app as provided by the brand.
